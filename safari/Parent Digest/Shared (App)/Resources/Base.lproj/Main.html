Parent Digest

What changed in your Veracross parent portal: assignments, teacher feedback, attendance, class posts and school messages.

Turn it on in Safari

  1. Open Safari and go to your school’s Veracross parent portal. Log in as usual.
  2. Tap the page menu at the left of the address bar, then Manage Extensions, and turn on Parent Digest.
  3. Allow it on the Veracross sites. Always Allow works best.
  4. Tap Parent Digest in the same menu to open your digest, then tap Refresh.

No Veracross account at hand? Open the digest and tap Try it with sample data.

You can turn on Parent Digest in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

Parent Digest is on. Log in to your Veracross parent portal in Safari, then click the Parent Digest button in the toolbar while you’re on a portal page.

Parent Digest is off. Turn it on in the Extensions section of Safari Settings, and allow it on the Veracross sites.

No Veracross account at hand? Open the digest and click Try it with sample data.

Everything stays on this device. Parent Digest has no server, no analytics and never sees your password. Not affiliated with Veracross.
