**Description:**
```
Parent Digest shows what changed in your school's Veracross parent portal at a glance, without clicking through every class.

• Action items: new attendance entries, changed due dates, and class posts or school messages that mention deadlines ("waiver needed by Thursday", "RSVP by Friday").
• A feed for each child: new, changed and removed assignments (with before → after), teacher feedback shown word for word, attendance, class posts and school messages.
• What's due in the next few days.
• "Mark all as seen", so next time you only see what's new.

Parent Digest is a Safari extension. Turn it on in Safari, log in to your school's Veracross parent portal as usual, then open Parent Digest from Safari's page menu (iPhone and iPad) or toolbar (Mac) and tap Refresh. Want to look first? Tap "Try it with sample data".

Private by design:
• Uses the login you already have in Safari. It never asks for, sees or stores your password.
• Everything stays on your device. No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking.
• Only talks to your school's Veracross portal.

Parent Digest is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Veracross. It reads the parent portal the same way Safari does, so a change to the portal can break it until the app is updated.
```

## App Privacy

**Data Not Collected.** Apple counts data as collected only when it's sent off the device. Parent Digest keeps everything in Safari's extension storage on the device and sends nothing to the developer or anyone else.

Parent Digest is a Safari Web Extension for parents whose school uses the Veracross parent portal (a school information system). Real use requires a parent account at a Veracross school; accounts are issued by schools to families, so we can't provide one.

To review without an account, use the built-in sample data:

Privacy: no account, no server, no analytics or tracking. The extension only contacts the Veracross portal sites listed in its manifest.
