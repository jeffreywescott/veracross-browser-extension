# Privacy Policy — Parent Digest for Veracross

_Last updated: September 24, 2026_

Parent Digest for Veracross ("the extension") is a browser extension that shows parents what changed in their school's Veracross parent portal. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Veracross.

## What the extension reads

When you press **Refresh**, or on an automatic check if you turn that on, the extension reads pages and data from your school's Veracross parent portal. It reads them the same way your browser does when you visit the portal, using the login you already have there. This includes:

- your children's names and classes
- assignments, due dates, completion status, scores and teacher notes
- teacher feedback
- attendance entries and class posts
- school messages and newsletters

It only contacts these three sites: `portals.veracross.com`, `portals-embed.veracross.com` and `classes.veracross.com`. The extension's own security policy blocks it from contacting any other site.

## What the extension stores

Everything is stored **only in your browser on your own device** (`chrome.storage.local`):

- the most recent copy of the data above
- the copy you last marked as seen, used to work out what's new
- short summaries of your last 12 digests
- your settings

## What the extension does not do

- It does **not** send any of this data anywhere. There is no server, no analytics, no tracking and no remote logging.
- It does **not** ask for, see or store your Veracross password. If your session has expired, it asks you to log in to Veracross yourself.
- It does **not** sell, share or transfer data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or any purpose other than showing you your digest.
- It does **not** sync data between devices.

Optional translation, where available, uses your browser's built-in on-device translation. Text is not sent to a translation service.

## Deleting your data

Open the extension's dashboard and choose **Settings → Delete all stored data**, or remove the extension from your browser. Either one deletes everything it stored.
